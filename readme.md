# Sonithic IRIS

### Helping Humans Hire Humans

**IRIS (Intelligent Recruitment Information System)** is an exploration into capability-based talent discovery and recruitment intelligence.

Modern hiring systems excel at identifying exact matches. They are often less effective at recognizing transferable capability across industries, organizations, and job titles.

IRIS explores whether AI can help bridge that gap.

Not by replacing recruiters.

Not by automating hiring decisions.

But by helping organizations recognize demonstrated capability wherever it exists.


## Status

**Concept & Architectural Prototype**

IRIS is currently in the design and discovery phase.

The project is focused on refining the underlying capability model, evaluation methodology, user experience, and integration strategy before implementation begins.

At this stage, the primary goal is to validate the problem, gather feedback from recruiting professionals, hiring managers, and talent leaders, and explore how AI can support better candidate visibility and discovery.


## The Problem

Organizations are not suffering from a shortage of information.

They are suffering from a shortage of visibility.

Modern recruiting workflows often rely on titles, keywords, certifications, and industry-specific terminology to identify potential candidates. While effective at scale, these approaches can unintentionally overlook individuals whose experience is highly relevant but described using a different professional vocabulary.

A construction manager, military logistics officer, enterprise sales leader, operations director, and program manager may all possess overlapping capabilities:

- Stakeholder Management

- Resource Coordination

- Leadership

- Risk Mitigation

- Executive Communication

- Strategic Planning

- Budget Oversight

- Cross-Functional Execution

Yet traditional hiring workflows frequently treat them as unrelated because their titles and terminology differ.

**Talent is often lost in translation.**


## Our Mission

**Helping Humans Hire Humans.**

IRIS is designed to help recruiters and hiring managers identify demonstrated capability rather than relying exclusively on title similarity or keyword matching.

The objective is not to make hiring decisions.

The objective is to help qualified candidates become visible.

By helping organizations understand what a candidate has actually demonstrated—not simply what titles they have held—IRIS seeks to support better hiring conversations and better hiring outcomes.


## Design Philosophy

IRIS is built around a simple premise:

> Experience should be translated into capability.

Rather than asking:

> Does this candidate have the exact title?

IRIS asks:

> What has this candidate actually done?

The system is intended to evaluate evidence of capabilities such as:

- Leadership

- Stakeholder Management

- Planning & Execution

- Risk Mitigation

- Resource Coordination

- Executive Communication

- Strategic Thinking

- Process Improvement

- Technical Adaptability

- Organizational Influence

These capabilities often transcend industries, organizations, and job titles.


## Explainable by Design

IRIS is intended to provide evidence-based reasoning rather than opaque scoring.

Instead of simply producing a ranking, the system should be able to answer questions such as:

- Why was this candidate surfaced?

- What evidence supports this assessment?

- Which capabilities appear transferable?

- What potential gaps exist?

- What areas should be explored during an interview?

The goal is to assist human judgment, not replace it.


## Human-in-the-Loop

IRIS is not designed to automatically hire, reject, or rank candidates without oversight.

Recruiters, hiring managers, and organizations remain responsible for all employment decisions.

IRIS is intended to function as a decision-support system that helps surface information, identify patterns, and highlight potentially overlooked candidates.

Human judgment remains central to the hiring process.


## Privacy First

Privacy is not a feature at Sonithic.

It is a design principle.

Whenever practical, Sonithic products are designed to process information locally and retain only the information necessary to provide their intended functionality.

For locally hosted solutions, Sonithic's preferred approach is simple:

- Your data remains under your control.

- Your data remains in your environment.

- Sonithic should not require access to your content to provide value.

Diagnostic information such as configuration files, crash logs, and troubleshooting data should remain under the control of the end user whenever possible.

Where support, licensing, cloud functionality, or future services require the transmission or storage of limited operational data, Sonithic will strive to collect only what is necessary to provide the requested service.

**Your data belongs to you.**

Sonithic's role is to help you use it—not take ownership of it.

## Relationship to Story Engine

IRIS builds upon several architectural concepts originally explored during the development of Story Engine.

While the domains are very different, both systems share common challenges:

- Information Retrieval

- Context Management

- Relevance Ranking

- Evidence-Based Reasoning

- Human-in-the-Loop Decision Support

Story Engine asks:

> What information is relevant to this story?

IRIS asks:

> What experience is relevant to this role?

Both seek to identify meaningful signal within large volumes of information.
